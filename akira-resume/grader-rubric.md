# Grader rubric (adapted)

Source: docs/ats-scoring.md from github.com/saranya-sallagundla/resume-builder-skill (copied in source-rubric.md).

There is no job posting yet, so dimensions 1-3 (keyword coverage, placement, title alignment)
and 7-8 (scope/progression across roles, page length) are skipped. That leaves 45 points:

| Dimension | Points | How to score |
|---|---|---|
| Parse safety | 10 | Start at 10. -3 table/columns, -2 header/footer content, -2 non-standard section names, -2 icons/graphics |
| Quantification | 15 | (bullets with a number OR a [#] placeholder / total bullets) x 15. Also report the score if placeholders stay unfilled. |
| Bullet quality | 10 | Start at 10. -1 per fluff verb (responsible for, helped, assisted, worked on, involved in, participated in), -1 per passive sentence, -1 per bullet over 2 lines (~200 chars), -1 per repeated opening verb in consecutive bullets |
| Humanization | 5 | Start at 5. -1 per cliche (spearheaded, leveraged, synergy, seamlessly, passionate, results-driven, proven track record, dynamic, cutting-edge, detail-oriented, team player); -2 if summary could belong to anyone |
| Hygiene | 5 | -1 per typo, inconsistent tense, date format, or punctuation issue |

Plus two pass/fail checks added for clinical work:
- **Truthfulness:** every claim traces to the source description. No invented numbers, tools, or duties.
- **Clinical scope:** a psychometrist/clinical ops associate supports evaluations. Fail if any line implies she
  diagnosed, interpreted, or independently conducted evaluations, or names/identifies any patient or case.
